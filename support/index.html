Getting started

First steps

Choose how you work (in your workshop, on site, or both), then add your business details in Account. Add a job with Jobs › +, and start the timer on it from Today.

Quotes and invoices

Open a job and tap Send quote or Create invoice. Preview shows the A4 page as it will print; Send shares it as a PDF.

Mileage

Put your workshop on the map in your business details, and give jobs an address. When you mark a collection, delivery, work on site or meeting as done, the trip is logged for you, with the driving distance from Apple Maps. Several stops on one day can be joined into one route in Money › Mileage.

Your records

Where are my records kept?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if backups are on (Account › Settings › Backup). We don’t have a copy and can’t see them.

Moving to a new iPhone

Sign in to the same iCloud on the new iPhone and install Time in Pocket; it offers to bring back your latest backup. You can also restore from Account › Settings › Backup.

Export everything

Account › Settings › Backup › Export everything makes one file with spreadsheets of your jobs, customers, invoices, payments, expenses, mileage and time, your photos and receipts, and a backup file. The spreadsheets open in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, without the app.

Coming from another app

Account › Help & your data › Import from another app brings in invoices and customers from Invoice Simple, or from a spreadsheet.

Deleting your data

Account › Help & your data › Privacy & terms › Delete everything removes your records from the iPhone and the app’s backups in your iCloud. Export a backup first if you might want them later.

Reminders

Reminders for collections, deliveries, meetings and money owed are set in Account › Settings › Reminders. Break reminders are in Account › Settings › Break schedule.

If none arrive, check that notifications are allowed for the app in your iPhone’s Settings, and that Focus or silent mode isn’t hiding them.
