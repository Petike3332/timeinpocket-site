Timer

See what every job really pays

Start the clock when you start work. The ring fills as your time adds up against the quote, so you can see at a glance how much of the price is left. It turns red if you go over.

  • Your day’s hours and what they’re worth, as you go
  • Paid and unpaid breaks, with reminders
  • Hours worked by day, week, month and year
  • Forgot to press Start? Add or fix the time afterwards

Jobs and customers

Every job, from first call to paid

Enquiry, booked, working, ready, delivered, paid. Each job shows where it stands, what’s next and when.

  • Workshop jobs, jobs at the customer’s, or both
  • Reminders for collections, deliveries and meetings
  • Before and after photos on every job
  • Customers save themselves, with Call, Message and Directions a tap away

Quotes and invoices

Paperwork that looks the part

Turn a job into a quote, and a quote into an invoice. Add your logged time and materials, then send it as a PDF straight from your phone.

  • Deposits and part payments
  • Due dates, with a reminder when one is overdue
  • With VAT if you’re registered, without if you’re not
  • A statement of everything a customer owes

Money

Know where the money is

Work done, profit, what’s owed to you and what’s booked, for any week, month or year. Tap a figure to see the jobs and invoices behind it.

  • Who owes you, with anything overdue in red
  • Money received, split by cash, bank transfer and card
  • An estimate of what to set aside for tax, at your own percentage
  • A year-end pack for your accountant

Business report

Your month on one page

One tap on the Money screen turns any month, year or tax year into a clear one-page report, in plain English, ready to save or send as a PDF.

  • Work done, money received, what you’re owed and your profit
  • Compared with the month or year before
  • Your real hourly rate, with admin and travel time counted
  • Who still owes you, and what the money was spent on

Questions

Good to know

When can I get it?

Soon. It’s nearly ready, and this page will say as soon as it’s on the App Store.

Is it only for iPhone?

Yes, for now. There’s no Android or iPad version yet.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. Your records are kept on your iPhone and backed up to your own iCloud.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Your jobs, timer and invoices are all on the phone, so it works in a cellar or on a site with no reception. Backups and map distances catch up when you’re back online.

I’m not VAT registered. Does that matter?

No. It works either way: with VAT on your invoices if you’re registered, without if you’re not.

Can I bring my records from another app?

Yes. Customers, invoices and expenses can be imported from a spreadsheet file.

What happens if I stop subscribing?

Nothing is deleted. You can still see, print, share and export everything. You just can’t add or change things until you subscribe again.

Is this tax advice?

No. The figures are there to help you keep track, and the tax figure is an estimate. Your accountant has the last word.
